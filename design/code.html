01 — CHALLENGE

Transforming fragmented brand presence into a coherent social-first narrative architecture.

The legacy brand communication architecture suffered from high-volume friction, platform saturation, and an absence of distinct narrative authority across key dynamic feeds. Rapid algorithm shifts diluted retention and fragmented consumer affinity. The assignment required engineering an uncompromising visual point of view capable of piercing through feed monotony, reasserting absolute premium brand posture, and constructing a sustainable high-velocity social framework without artistic compromise.

02 — STRATEGY

A disciplined four-pillar content framework calibrated for algorithmic retention and cultural authority.

PHASE I // CADENCE

High-density algorithmic calibration prioritizing 3-second hook conversion and ruthless pacing across 9:16 vertical real estate.

PHASE II // MONOGRAPH

Editorial 4:5 carousels designed as tactile publications inside the feed, driving prolonged dwell time and save metrics.

PHASE III // CINEMA

Anamorphic long-form documentary cuts exploring craft, materials, and cultural friction for community retention.
